# Why Workday Adaptive Planning, checked 10 October 2026

Workday Adaptive Planning is planning, budgeting and forecasting software for finance teams: versions of the P&L, balance sheet and cash, workforce plans, and the reports the board sees. Budgeting and forecasting was not yet a family in the rebuild registry, and it is the work Enterprise DNA's own finance reporting and Power BI background sits closest to.

**The bill.** Workday publishes no price for Adaptive Planning; every plan is quoted. Vendr's [Workday buyer guide](https://www.vendr.com/buyer-guides/workday) puts Adaptive Planning on its own at roughly $5,900 to $25,100 a year, and as an add-on for an organisation of about 200 employees at $18,000 to $31,200. [AutomationAtlas](https://automationatlas.io/answers/workday-adaptive-planning-pricing-2026/) (July 2026) estimates mid-market entry at $30,000 to $60,000 a year for a single module and 25 to 75 planners. These are third-party estimates of quotes, not invoices, and none of them includes the implementation partner who builds the model.

**Scores, each out of five.** Annual bill 4 (tens of thousands a year at mid-market, quote only). Data-shaped 5 (entities, accounts, versions, monthly values, hires, covenants, commentary, close tasks, lodgements, distributions). Our buyers 5 (every NZ and AU business of 20 to 500 staff with a finance lead, and accounting firms selling outsourced CFO work; no customer-brain or CRM reference to Adaptive Planning was checked in this run). Search demand 3 ("Adaptive Planning alternative" and FP&A software searches are answered by listicles and rival vendors; no keyword volume was measured). One-session buildable 5. Import path 5 (Integration > Export Data writes a documented CSV of account values by level and month). Total 27 of 30.

**Honest limits.** Adaptive Planning's strengths are a spreadsheet-like grid many budget holders type into at once, driver-based models, and consolidation across currencies. This build keeps the numbers and the monthly routine; drivers, consolidation and a grid for budget holders are customisation work.
