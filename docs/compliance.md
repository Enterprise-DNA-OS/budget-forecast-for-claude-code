# Record checks and their sources

`/compliance` (`npm run plan -- compliance`) checks the records against the rules below. It finds records that are missing, out of date or inconsistent. It does not decide whether a distribution is lawful, whether a return is correct, or what a facility agreement means. Those calls belong to the directors, the tax agent and the lawyers. Sources checked 10 October 2026.

| Rule | What the check finds | Source and limit |
|---|---|---|
| NZ-KIWISAVER | A filled or planned NZ role whose employer contribution rate is below the KiwiSaver employer minimum in force at any point while it is employed, inside the planning horizon (the last month of any budget or forecast) | Inland Revenue, [KiwiSaver changes](https://www.ird.govt.nz/updates/news-folder/2025/kiwisaver-changes): the minimum employer rate rises from 3% to 3.5% on 1 April 2026 and to 4% on 1 April 2028. An employee with an IRD-approved temporary rate reduction can stay at 3%: record the end date with `update-hire --reduced-rate-until`, and the check accepts 3% until then. Rates live in the `statutory_rates` table. |
| AU-SUPER-GUARANTEE | A filled or planned Australian role below the super guarantee rate in force while it is employed | The ATO's [key superannuation rates and thresholds](https://www.ato.gov.au/Rates/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/): 11.5% for 2024-25 and 12% from 1 July 2025. The check compares rates only; it does not test the maximum contribution base or Payday Super timing. |
| NZ-GST-DUE | An NZ GST return past its due date with no lodgement recorded | Inland Revenue, [filing and paying GST](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/filing-and-paying-gst-and-refunds): due on the 28th of the month after the period ends, except periods ending 31 March (due 7 May) and 30 November (due 15 January). IRD moves a due date on a weekend or public holiday to the next working day; this check does not, so it can flag a day or two early. Monthly and two-monthly periods are supported; two-monthly periods are assumed to end in even months (change it with `/customise`). |
| AU-BAS-DUE | An Australian BAS past its due date with no lodgement recorded | The ATO's [BAS due dates](https://www.ato.gov.au/BASduedates): monthly BAS on the 21st of the following month; quarterly BAS on 28 October, 28 February, 28 April and 28 July. Online lodgers and tax agents often get two more weeks on quarters 1, 3 and 4; this check uses the standard date, so it can flag early. |
| NZ-S52-CERTIFICATE | A declared or paid NZ distribution with no directors' solvency certificate recorded | [Companies Act 1993](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1993/0105/latest/whole.html) s52: the board may authorise a distribution only if satisfied on reasonable grounds that the company will meet the solvency test (s4) immediately after it, and the directors who vote for it sign a certificate. Record the signing date with `/certify`. The check confirms a date is on file, not that the test was met. |
| AU-S254T-NET-ASSETS | A proposed or declared Australian dividend larger than net assets at the last closed month, or with no actual balance sheet on file | [Corporations Act 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00818/latest/text) s254T: a company must not pay a dividend unless its assets exceed its liabilities immediately before it is declared and the excess is sufficient for the payment, the payment is fair and reasonable to shareholders as a whole, and it does not materially prejudice its ability to pay its creditors. Net assets here are the management balance sheet; the directors use the figures as at the declaration date. |
| SOLVENCY-CASH | A proposed or declared distribution after which forecast closing cash goes below zero in any of the next twelve months | Supports the cash limb of the NZ solvency test (s4(1)(a): able to pay debts as they become due) and the creditor limb of s254T(1)(c). The check subtracts the payment from forecast cash unless `--in-forecast=true` says the forecast already includes it. |
| BUDGET-APPROVAL | A budget whose first month has started that nobody has approved and locked | Internal governance. Nothing is judged against an unapproved budget: the month-end uses the newest approved budget. |
| COVENANT-BREACH | A covenant whose last test on actual figures failed | The facility agreement. Interest cover and debt to EBITDA use trailing twelve months of EBITDA from the accounts tagged in the chart; minimum cash uses closing cash. Lenders define EBITDA and debt in the agreement, often with adjustments this does not make. |

The attention list (`/attention`) adds operating checks with no legal source: variances over the thresholds in the `settings` table without a comment, overdue close tasks, a forecast covenant breach, forecast cash below zero, a tax return due within 14 days, a forecast still carrying forecast figures for closed months, and an unapproved budget.

There is no delete command. Removing a record is a deliberate database change made by the operator, recorded in the activity log.
